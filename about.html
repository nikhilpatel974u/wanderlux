A little about us

We believe the best trips
feel personal.

WanderLux is a travel agency for curious people who want a little more meaning, care and ease in the way they explore.

Travel is about how a place makes you feel.

Our approach

Less checklist.
More connection.

We started WanderLux with a simple idea: travel planning should feel as considered as the trip itself. That means listening carefully, suggesting experiences that fit, and making the practical details easier to manage.

We work with travellers to build journeys that balance discovery and downtime—from a first international holiday to a long-awaited celebration.
